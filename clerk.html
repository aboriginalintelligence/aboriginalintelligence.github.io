Limits

What this desk will not do

  • It will not guess a section number.
  • It will not treat the drafts as adopted law.
  • It will not take a complaint about a named person.
  • It will not replace a lawyer before a vote, a membership decision, or a court step.

Sources include the Indian Act, the First Nations Financial Transparency Act, the First Nations Fiscal Management Act, the First Nations Elections Act, Treaty 8, and the documents on this site. Justice consolidations in the library are current to 30 September 2026.
