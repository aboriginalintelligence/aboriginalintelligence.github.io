No. 01

Candidate Commitment

Agreement

Cite this instrument as No. 01, Candidate Commitment.

Summary

A candidate reads the codes, signs this page, and gives members a signed copy.

The codes do nothing until members adopt them. A candidate who will follow them can say so in writing before the vote, and can agree to resign if they do not follow through.

Part 1. What this is

1.1 A candidate signs this commitment.

1.2 A candidate who signs it will follow it if they take a seat, and agrees to resign if they do not.

Part 2. What the candidate does

2.1 If you are running, do these steps yourself.

  • Download this commitment yourself.
  • Read it, and read the codes it names.
  • Commit to it.
  • Endorse it in your own words.
  • Sign it and date it.
  • Promote it.
  • Make it public.
  • Make a video of yourself saying you signed it and you mean it.
  • Give a signed copy to members.

Part 3. The commitment

3.1 I have read the constitution, the bill of rights, the election code, the council procedure, the conduct instrument, the band money instrument, and the spending instrument in this set.

3.2 I will support a member vote on those instruments. I will not ask council to adopt them by resolution alone.

3.3 I will not vote on my own pay, honorarium, travel, or a contract with me or my immediate family. I will leave the room.

3.4 I will not commit more than $10,000 of the First Nation's money by myself. I will not sign a contract over $25,000 unless a written invitation to bid was posted for 14 days. I will follow the signature rules and the publication of the financial statements and the remuneration schedule.

3.5 I will not vote to postpone the fixed election day.

3.6 I will disclose conflicts within the days the conduct instrument sets, and I will accept the appeal body as the place a member reports a breach.

3.7 Within 30 days of taking office I will file a public mandate of 3 to 5 goals, each with a number, a date, or a defined outcome. Within 30 days after each quarter I will file a public report on those goals.

3.8 I will not vote to cancel, postpone, or defund a removal petition or a removal vote that meets the election code.

3.9 If I take office and I do not follow this commitment, I agree to resign. I will deliver records, keys, and passwords within 7 days of resigning.

3.10 Signature, printed name, and date.
