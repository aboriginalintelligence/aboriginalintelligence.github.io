Agreement 02

Oath

Not a statute. The duties it names sit in the codes, or in the rules already in force.Comments welcome. A comment is public. It is not a vote and does not change the draft.

In plain words

  • Said out loud at a council meeting and signed.
  • No vote counts until it's signed.
  • Posted for members within 2 days.

Why this draft is here

Every person who holds the Nation's money and decisions should say, in public and in writing, what they owe members before they cast a vote.

Read the full text Download the print version

Part 1. What this is

1.1

These are the words a person says out loud at a council meeting, and then signs, before voting as chief or councillor.

1.2

The council procedure says a person who has not signed must not vote. A vote cast before signing is void.

1.3

The records officer posts the signed oath within 2 days.

1.4

The oath is not a statute. The duties it names are in the codes. If the codes are not yet in force, the person is promising to follow the election rule and the accountability rules that are in force, and not to block a member vote on the drafts.

Part 2. The words

2.1

I accept this office as a representative of every member, on reserve and off reserve. I work for the members.

2.2

I will follow the election rules in force and every code the members have adopted.

2.3

I will put the First Nation's interest ahead of my own and ahead of my immediate family's when I deal with the First Nation's money, land, housing, jobs, or programs.

2.4

I will disclose a conflict within 5 days and I will leave the room. I will not vote on my own pay, honorarium, travel, or contract.

2.5

I will not commit more than $10,000 alone. I will spend money only as the spending rules allow.

2.6

I will post my goals within 30 days of taking office and a report on them within 30 days after each quarter.

2.7

I will answer a member's written question on council business within 30 days.

2.8

I will not treat a house, a job, or a payment as a personal favour.

2.9

When my term ends I will hand over the office within 7 days, including records, keys, devices, and passwords.

2.10

If I do not keep this oath, I agree to resign.
