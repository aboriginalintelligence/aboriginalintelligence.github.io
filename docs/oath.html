Part 1. What this is

1.1 These are the words a person says out loud, and then signs, before voting as chief or councillor. The council procedure says a person who has not signed must not vote.

1.2 The oath is not itself a statute. The duties it names are in the instruments. If those instruments are not yet in force, the person is promising to follow the election rule and the accountability rules that are in force, and not to obstruct a member vote on the drafts.

Part 2. The words

2.1 I am a member of this First Nation. I will follow the election rules in force, and any code the members have adopted.

2.2 I will put the First Nation's interest ahead of my own interest and ahead of my immediate family when I am using the First Nation's money, housing, or jobs.

2.3 I will disclose a conflict within 5 days and I will leave the room. I will not vote on my own pay, honorarium, travel, or contract.

2.4 I will not spend money except as the spending rule allows. I will not treat a house, a job, or a payment as a personal favour.

2.5 When my term ends, I will hand over the office within 7 days, including records, keys, devices, and passwords.

2.6 If I do not follow this oath, I agree to resign.

2.7 Signature, printed name, and date, witnessed by the records officer.
