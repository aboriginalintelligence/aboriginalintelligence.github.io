Rules first. Then the vote.

These are working drafts of the rules a First Nation government runs on: elections, money, contracts, housing, information, and complaints.

Each one is written so no one person can spend, hire, or change the rules alone. Nothing here is law until members vote it in.

Working drafts. Not adopted law. Not legal advice.The rules already in force stay in force until members replace them the way those rules require. A lawyer should review any text before a vote.

Codes

14 drafts that work together as one set

Code 03

Constitution

Sets council, the fixed election day, the ban on voting on your own pay, the protected rules, and how members, not council alone, adopt and change the codes.

Read the draftCode 04

Bill of rights

The same vote, the same right to information, and the same program rules for every member, on reserve or off reserve.

Read the draftCode 05

Elections

Five seats, one of them off reserve. Every elector votes for every seat. An independent electoral officer the electors pick. Mail ballots for everyone. Removal takes a 40 percent petition, then a vote.

Read the draftCode 06

Council procedure

Meet every month. Agenda out 7 days before. Minutes posted in 7 days. Goals posted within 30 days of taking office and reported on every quarter.

Read the draftCode 07

Conduct and conflict of interest

A public conflict register within 5 days, leave the room before debate, a $100 gift limit, and removal after a second missed recusal.

Read the draftCode 08

Membership

Takes no one off the current list. It is the path if electors choose to take control of membership under Indian Act section 10, with a written decision and an appeal.

Read the draftCode 09

Band money

A posted budget members can comment on, a plain quarterly statement, and the audited statements and pay schedule within 120 days of year end.

Read the draftCode 10

Spending and contracts

$10,000 or less needs two signatures. More than $10,000 needs a council vote. More than $25,000 also needs a public call for bids. A monthly contract list for members.

Read the draftCode 11

Housing

One posted waiting list with fixed points, a written offer, the same rent for the same unit, and no lock change without notice, 30 days, and a hearing.

Read the draftCode 12

Education support

One education budget, a written decision in 30 days, the same rate for every member in the same situation, and one appeal.

Read the draftCode 13

Ventures and member loans

Three separate pots: personal loans up to $5,000, business help up to $10,000 a year, and Nation businesses with their own books posted every quarter.

Read the draftCode 14

Right to information

Ask in writing, get an answer in 30 days, a short closed list of reasons to refuse, and an appeal the next day if the deadline is missed.

Read the draftCode 15

Whistleblower protection

A report goes straight to the appeal body, not up the chain of command. Anonymous reports allowed. Any job loss, eviction, or program cut within 180 days is presumed to be reprisal unless written reasons are filed in 10 days.

Read the draftCode 16

Appeals and complaints

Three electors, not on council and not staff, decide complaints in writing on fixed deadlines. Council cannot overturn them.
