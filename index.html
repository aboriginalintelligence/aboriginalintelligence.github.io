Candidate commitment

The commitment is not a law. It does not put anyone in office. A candidate downloads it, reads the codes, signs it, and gives members a signed copy.

If the candidate later takes a seat and does not follow the commitment, the candidate agrees to resign.

  1. Download the commitment yourself.
  2. Read it.
  3. Commit to it.
  4. Endorse it.
  5. Sign it.
  6. Promote it.
  7. Make it public.
  8. Make a video of yourself saying you signed it and you mean it.
  9. Give a signed copy to members.

Agreements

  • Candidate commitment
    A personal commitment to the member controls in the drafts. It is not a law and it does not put anyone in office.
  • Oath
    The words a chief or councillor says and signs before voting at council.

Codes

  • Constitution
    Sets council, the fixed election day, the ban on voting on your own pay, and the rule that members, not council alone, can amend it.
  • Bill of rights
    The same vote, the same information right, and the same program rules for every member, on reserve or off reserve.
  • Elections
    One chief, two councillors, a vote for every elector, an electoral officer the council does not appoint, and a day council cannot move.
  • Council procedure
    Monthly meetings, 7 days' notice, minutes in 7 days, and no spending decision by text message.
  • Conduct and conflict of interest
    A public conflict register within days, recusal before debate, and removal after a second missed recusal.
  • Membership
    Does not take anyone off the current list. It is the section 10 path if electors later assume control, with a written decision and an appeal.
  • Band money
    A posted budget, a quarterly statement, and the audited statements and remuneration schedule within 120 days of year end.
  • Spending and contracts
    Two signatures under $2,500, a resolution over $25,000, public notice over $100,000, and a member vote over $500,000.
  • Housing
    One posted waiting list with fixed points, a written offer, and no lock change until notice, 30 days, and a hearing are confirmed.
  • Education support
    One budget line, a written decision in 30 days, the same rate for every member in the same row, and one appeal.
  • Ventures and member loans
    Three pots: a personal loan capped at $5,000, business help capped at $10,000 in a year, and a Nation venture with its own books.
  • Right to information
    A written request, an answer in 30 days, a closed list of exceptions, and an appeal the next day if the deadline is missed.
  • Whistleblower protection
    A report goes to the appeal body, not up the chain of command. Reprisal within 180 days is presumed unless written reasons are filed in 10 days.
  • Appeals and complaints
    Three electors, not on council, decide complaints in writing. Council cannot overturn them.
